About the Farm

Hollow Creek Farm has been in the Calloway family since 1962. What started as a small vegetable operation has grown into one of the region's most beloved agritainment destinations.

Founders Bill and Margaret Calloway passed the farm to their daughter, Dana, and her husband, Rob, in 2008. Dana and Rob's kids, Tyler, Meg, and Sam, grew up on the farm and now help run it full time.

The farm sits on 140 acres and includes working fields, a restored 1890s barn, a sunflower field, a corn maze, a pick-your-own pumpkin patch, a small orchard, and a covered pavilion for events.

Visitors often describe Hollow Creek Farm as the kind of place that makes you slow down.
