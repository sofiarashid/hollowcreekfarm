About Us

Hollow Creek Farm is a family-owned and operated farm located in the heart of the countryside. We are dedicated to providing fresh, locally grown produce to our community while promoting sustainable farming practices. Our farm has been in operation for over 20 years, and we take pride in our commitment to quality and environmental stewardship.

At Hollow Creek Farm, we grow a wide variety of fruits and vegetables, including heirloom tomatoes, sweet corn, strawberries, and leafy greens. We also raise free-range chickens for eggs and pasture-raised beef. Our farm is open to the public during the growing season, where visitors can pick their own produce and learn about sustainable farming practices.

We believe in supporting our local community and strive to create a welcoming environment for all. Whether you're a seasoned gardener or just looking for fresh, delicious produce, we invite you to visit Hollow Creek Farm and experience the beauty of farm life.
